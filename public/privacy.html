Privacy notice

TopDraw Projects uses the details you send through this website only to deal with your enquiry. This notice explains how, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form we collect your name, phone number, email address, suburb, the type of space and project, and your message.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to quote and deliver your project. We do not sell your details.

Who handles it

Your enquiry is emailed to us through an email delivery service (Resend) and is stored by our website host (Netlify) so that no enquiry is lost.

How long

We keep your details only for as long as we need them for your enquiry or project.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing trevor@topdrawprojects.co.za.

Complaints

You can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.
