# PRODUCT.md — Top Draw

## One line
Top Draw designs, builds and installs high-end bespoke cabinetry, furniture and interiors in Johannesburg. One team, led by one craftsman, from site measure to final completion.

## Owner
Trevor, owner and lead craftsman. Former boilermaker and sheet metal worker (stainless steel fabrication for food and beverage), then architectural metalwork specialising in custom balustrading, then high-end furniture, bespoke cabinetry and interior design. He brings an engineer's tolerances to joinery. Hands-on. Gets it right the first time. Goes the extra mile.

## Services
1. Full turnkey projects
2. General carpentry
3. Kitchen and bathroom design
4. Shell prep for new cabinetry installation
5. Project management

## Positioning
The differentiator is provenance of precision: Trevor learned to work in stainless steel, where a millimetre out means it does not fit. The brand idea is **the shop drawing**: the technical document every bespoke piece starts from. It signals that the work is measured, specified and accountable.

## Voice
South African English (specialise, colour, centre, metre). Confident, plain, specific. Short sentences. Talk in materials, dimensions and stages, not adjectives.

Never: em dashes, "elevate", "seamless", "unlock", "transform your space", "dream", "passion", "world-class".
